Meal Tracking made simple

Select * From Meal helps people organize their meals, understand their nutrition, and create better eating habits

About us

Select * From Meals is a software company aim to help people organize their daily meals and better understand their nutritional habits. Our applications exist as a simple way to record food and manage meal entries while reviewing nutrional information

Our mission

Our mission is to make Meal tracking simple and accessible for everyone by helping them organize their food records and make more careful decisions when going about their eating habits

The problem

People have difficulty keeping track of what they eat every day. Not only recording meal manually can be time consuming, but understanding nutritional information can be confusing. Without an organized record, people will struggle to understand their eating patterns

Our solution

Select * From Meals allows users to record their meal type, including breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Users can create, view, edit their meal entries and review their nutritional information through a dashboard

Meet our member

We are a team of six member working together to design and develop Select * From Meal. Each member contributes to the planning, development, and documentation of the project
